1.Defining problem statement¶

The objective of this project is to develop a ML model that predicts whether a credict card customer will misss the next months payment.This helps financial institutions identify high-risk customers early and take preventive actions to minimize financial losses.

2. Identify:¶

a. Which is more costly? False Positive or False Negative?¶

**False Negative (more dangerous-Financial loss)

Actual situation: Customer misses payment,(High risk=1)

Model prediction: Customer is safe

**False Positive (less dangerous-Customer Dissatisfaction)

Actual situation: Customer pays normally,(low risk=0)

Model prediction: Customer is risky

False negatives are more costly because predicting a risky customer as low risk may lead to financial loss for the bank.False positives may only cause minor customer dissatisfaction.

3. Choose the most appropriate evaluation metric and justify your choice¶

Recall is chosen as the primary evaluation metric because it measures the model’s ability to correctly identify risky customers and minimize false negatives.
